Battleship is a local naval combat game for Android. It requires no network connection, no account and no tracking. Play against an AI opponent that uses a hunt/target algorithm with checkerboard parity, or pass the device to a friend for two-player mode.

The game is played on a 10x10 grid with a 5-ship fleet: Carrier, Battleship, Cruiser, Submarine and Destroyer. Ships are placed by tapping a cell, tapping again to rotate, or deploying the whole fleet with one tap. Sinking an enemy ship unlocks a matching super weapon, such as area bombardment, cross-pattern saturation fire or a sonar sweep.

The app tracks career stats across games and app restarts, including 33 medals across four rarity tiers and 8 career ranks from Cadet to Fleet Admiral. All game audio is synthesized at runtime, so no audio files are bundled with the app.
