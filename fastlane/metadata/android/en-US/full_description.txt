Battleship is a naval strategy game for Android. You place your fleet, then you and the computer take turns firing at each other's ships. Sink all five enemy ships to win. The game works offline: it needs no network connection and no account, and it shows no ads and does not track you.

The computer searches alternating squares of the grid. After a hit, it fires at the squares around it to find the rest of the ship.

The game is played on a 10x10 grid. Each side has five ships: Carrier, Battleship, Cruiser, Submarine and Destroyer. To place a ship, tap a square. A separate button turns the ship between horizontal and vertical, and AUTO DEPLOY places the whole fleet at random with one tap. When you sink an enemy ship, you unlock one matching super weapon, a special attack you can use once, such as Carpet Bomb (a 3x3 area), Battleship Barrage (a cross of 9 squares) or Sonar Sweep (a row of 5 squares).

Sound effects mark hits, misses, sunken ships and victories.

The app keeps your game record on your phone: your statistics, 33 medals in four rarity levels and 8 career ranks from Cadet to Fleet Admiral. The record is kept between games and when you close the app. The app is available in English and Danish.
