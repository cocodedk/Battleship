BATTLESHIP

◆ CLASSIFIED — MISSION BRIEFING

HOW IT WORKS

Battleship is played on a 10×10 grid against the computer. Each side has five ships: a Carrier, a Battleship, a Cruiser, a Submarine and a Destroyer. You cannot see where the computer's ships are.

Place your five ships by tapping squares on the grid, or tap AUTO DEPLOY to place them at random. Then tap INITIATE COMBAT. You and the computer take turns firing at one square each. To fire, tap a square in TARGET ZONE. Sink all five enemy ships to win.

Each enemy ship you sink unlocks a super weapon, a special attack you can use once: Carpet Bomb, Battleship Barrage, Sonar Sweep, Torpedo Spread or Precision Strike. Every battle adds to your record of statistics, medals and ranks, from Cadet to Fleet Admiral.

◆ ARSENAL — SUPER WEAPONS CLEARED FOR USE

SUPER WEAPONS

💣
CARPET BOMB
Attacks up to 9 cells in a 3×3 square centred on your target. The pattern stops at the board edge.
Unlocked by sinking the enemy CARRIER
💥
BATTLESHIP BARRAGE
Attacks up to 9 cells in a plus shape: 5 across and 5 down, crossing at your target. The pattern stops at the board edge.
Unlocked by sinking the enemy BATTLESHIP
📡
SONAR SWEEP
Attacks a row of 5 cells centred on your target. The pattern stops at the board edge.
Unlocked by sinking the enemy CRUISER
🐠
TORPEDO SPREAD
Attacks a column of 5 cells centred on your target. The pattern stops at the board edge.
Unlocked by sinking the enemy SUBMARINE
🎯
PRECISION STRIKE
Attacks 5 cells in an X shape centred on your target. The pattern stops at the board edge, so in a corner it hits only 2 cells.
Unlocked by sinking the enemy DESTROYER

SENSES OF WAR

💥
HIT FLASH
A white flash appears on a cell you hit and fades in half a second.

SUNK GLOW
The cells of a sunken ship pulse with a red glow, so you can see which ships are gone.
🟠
ENEMY TURN INDICATOR
The turn bar blinks amber and reads ENEMY CALCULATING... while the computer picks its next shot.

HIT SOUND
Two quick tones when a shot hits: 1800 Hz, then 1000 Hz.

🎺

WEAPON-SELECTION SOUND

◆ SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

TACTICAL OVERVIEW

◎

OFFLINE PLAY

No server, no network. Runs entirely on your device. Works anywhere, any time.

◈

COMPUTER OPPONENT

The computer searches alternating squares of the grid, then fires at the squares around a hit to find the rest of the ship.

⊞

10×10 TACTICAL GRID

A fleet of 5 ships on a full-size Battleship grid. Place them by hand or tap AUTO DEPLOY.

💣

5 SUPER WEAPONS

Carpet Bomb, Battleship Barrage, Sonar Sweep, Torpedo Spread and Precision Strike. Each unlocks when you sink a certain ship, and you can use it once.

🏅

33 MEDALS

Four rarity levels: Legendary, Epic, Rare and Common. MEDAL REGISTRY shows each medal and how many times you have earned it.

⭐

8 MILITARY RANKS

Cadet to Fleet Admiral. Your best score decides your rank.

🔊

SOUND EFFECTS

Sounds for hits, misses, sunken ships, victories and selecting a super weapon.

🚫

NO ADS · NO ACCOUNT

Free, open-source, Apache-2.0. No tracking, no sign-in, no paywalls.
